Tips:

To Update .Dat Files:

    1.	Run DNINTG (Ctrl-D) to download CIM Port/Sector reports.
        Get the CIM Port, Sector and 'V' screen, at least.
    2.  Run CRAI (Ctrl-C) to build/update a *.dat file from the *.prt file.

    Repeat the above sequence regularly until you build up a good database
    (*.dat files).  Step 2 can be done On or Offline.

Don't forget to switch between .KEY files with Alt-F2.
